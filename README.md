# Process-Optimisation-for-sustainable-cement-production-with-machine-learning
Research work conducted by: Amusat Haki Adeyemi


This project focuses on developing a machine learning-driven optimization framework for sustainable cement production. The framework integrates chemical engineering principles and employs a multi-objective optimization algorithm to balance energy efficiency, CO2 emissions reduction, and clinker production rate. The goal is to provide real-time recommendations for process adjustments that minimize environmental impact while maintaining or increasing production. The results demonstrate significant reductions in both energy consumption and CO2 emissions, highlighting the potential of this framework to enhance the sustainability and efficiency of cement production.
